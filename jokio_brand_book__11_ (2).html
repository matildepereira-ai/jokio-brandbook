01 / Positioning

The truth of the brand. In one breath.

Get this part right and the rest of the playbook is intuitive. Get it wrong and the brand has a regulatory problem before the creative ever has the chance to perform.

Jokio is the live horse racing wagering platform. Reimagined for the reveal.

An ORC-approved advance deposit wagering platform operated by Vision Derby as an affiliate of AmWest Entertainment. Every wager is placed on a real, upcoming horse race. Every outcome is decided by the race itself. The reveal, the visual moment a user gets to see how their wager landed, is a presentation layer. A way of telling the truth, not a way of generating it.

That is the entire story. It's also the entire compliance posture. The product is pari-mutuel wagering on live horse racing, regulated by the Oregon Racing Commission Multi-Jurisdictional Hub License. Speak about it that way and the brand stays standing. Speak about it any other way and we have a different conversation with our regulator.

Everything in this playbook flows from one phrase. Powered by Live Horse Racing. It is mandatory in every advertisement. It is also the most useful thing to remember when you're stuck.

✓ Jokio is
A live horse racing wagering platform with a stylised reveal experience.
ORC-licensed. Pari-mutuel. Operated by Vision Derby as an affiliate of AmWest. Every outcome is determined by the live race result, every reveal is a visual display of that verified outcome.
✗ Jokio is not
A casino. A slot product. An RNG-driven game. A sweepstakes.
The reveal interface may visually echo formats used elsewhere, but the words "Casino" and "Slot" are absolutely prohibited in Jokio marketing in any form, including compounds, hashtags, alt text, metadata, and video scripts. No defensive framings. No "the legal version of." The phrase doing the work is Powered by Live Horse Racing.
02 / Non-Negotiables

The six rules that ship every ad.

Every creative must clear all six. That includes banners, posts, emails, video frames, landing pages and influencer captions. There are no waivers, no exceptions, no "we'll fix it in V2."

Read these. Memorise them. Brief them in.

If a creative breaks any of the six, it doesn't get reviewed. It gets sent back.

Mandatory · ORC Affiliate Framework
01

Always include "Powered by Live Horse Racing."

Mandatory in every advertising unit, prominently and legibly. Headline, body or footer. Visible at all times. The single phrase that anchors the legal positioning and satisfies the ORC affiliate framework.

Required Phrase
02

Never use "Casino" or "Slot" in any form.

Absolute prohibition. Includes every compound: casino-style, online casino, slot machine, video slots, slot-themed, social casino, poker slots. Applies to copy, hashtags, alt text, metadata, video scripts. No exceptions.

Hard Prohibition
03

Never imply guaranteed wins or risk-free wagering.

Jokio is genuine pari-mutuel wagering. Users can win and users can lose. Avoid risk-free, can't lose, sure thing, earn, make money, investment, returns, profit. All claims factual, always.

Truth in Advertising
04

The reveal is a display, not a game of chance.

The visual doesn't decide the outcome. The live horse race does. Never describe the reveal as a casino game, an RNG product or a slot machine, even by visual implication. It's a stylised reveal of a verified race outcome.

Product Truth
05

Run the full mandatory footer in every ad.

If $100 offer is in ad Min $5, Max $100, 1x wagering, eligibility applies. Bonus non withdrawable.

Standard lineOffered by AmWest through their Oregon Multi-Jurisdictional Hub License. 21+. T&Cs apply. For help with a gambling problem, please call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Mandatory Footer
06

Every variant goes to Vision Derby before publication.

No creative goes live without written compliance approval. The chain is Vision Derby → ORC → written sign-off. Never publish first and seek approval after. That's grounds for partner suspension.

04 / Visual System

Typography. A two-family system.

Unbounded does the heavy lifting on display and headline tier. Inter handles everything else: subheads, body, UI, captions, meta. Contrast between the two families gives the brand a confident editorial voice without ever needing a third typeface.

Unbounded

Display & Headline

The display face. Rounded geometric grotesque with a slight techno character that gives Jokio its swagger at large sizes. Used for cover titles, section titles, manifesto headlines, USP statements, offer headlines, and the big numerals across the playbook.

Weight: Bold 700 as the workhorse. Treat 700 as the default and only deviate with written brand approval.

Inter

Subhead, Body & UI

The workhorse. Inter handles every subhead, every paragraph, every CTA label, every UI element and every caption. A modern grotesque designed for screens, sized down for affiliate banners and up for landing copy without a wobble.

Weights in regular use: 300 for body, 400 for default, 500–600 for subheads and meta, 700 for CTA labels.

07 / Comms Playbook

A working Do & Don't for marketing comms.

The Six Golden Rules tell you the line. This is how to stay on the right side of it across every channel, every brief, every audience and every state.

Do Don't
Do.
Behaviour to lean into.

Lean into live racing context, regulatory clarity, and emotional honesty. Strong brand work and strong compliance are the same work.

Language & Framing
  • Anchor every line in live horse racing. Use "live race", "upcoming race", "pari-mutuel", "advance deposit wagering".
  • Lead with the verified product story: place a wager → watch the race → reveal the result → withdraw winnings.
  • Describe the reveal as a "visual reveal", "race reveal", "stylised display". Language that makes clear it's a presentation layer.
  • Use approved verbs: place, wager, watch, reveal, win, withdraw, fund, play, discover, unlock.
  • Use precise legal phrasing: "Oregon Racing Commission Multi-Jurisdictional Hub License" or "ORC-licensed advance deposit wagering".
  • Ground "win" in real-cash, race-result outcomes. Never frame winnings as a yield, return, or pay-out from the platform itself.
Visual & Imagery
  • Use stylised racing imagery: riders, paddock, post, finish line, trophy, paddock cash, cup & silks.
  • Cast adult talent. Mature, entertainment-focused, premium-feeling. Always with adult appeal.
  • Use the supplied logo lockup unmodified. Always on a high-contrast field.
  • Lead with the magenta-to-orange spotlight gradient for hero CTAs and the live green for confirmation.
  • Keep "Powered by Live Horse Racing" prominently legible. Never below 11pt in long-form, never compressed in social.
Promotional Claims
  • State only factual, pre-approved promo terms. Wager amounts, jackpot values, prize figures, match percentages.
  • Reflect real T&Cs of any welcome credits or deposit-match offers. Show the conditions, don't bury them.
  • Use specific, verifiable numbers when you have them. Vague claims invite both consumer mistrust and regulatory scrutiny.
  • Use approved promo phrasing from Section 8.3 of the master document where possible.
Targeting & Geo
  • Age targeting: 21+. Verify per state before deployment.
  • For the most up-to-date list of states where we operate, please contact your Affiliate Account Manager.
  • Suppress underage interest categories, behavioural pools and child-adjacent placements across every platform.
  • For New York creative, always include the additional self-exclusion footer line, no exceptions.
Disclosure & Footer
  • Run the full mandatory footer in every ad unit. Provider Statement plus RG Statement.
  • Keep footer type legible. Never shrunk to noise to "make room" for creative.
Process & Hygiene
  • Submit every variant to Vision Derby compliance for written approval before publication.
  • Treat first-round notes as a partnership. Re-submit cleanly, and don't argue line-by-line.
  • Keep an internal version log of approved creative: assets, dates, approvers, and the ORC reference where applicable.
  • Audit existing creative when guidelines update; remove or amend non-conforming assets within the agreed SLA.
Don't.
Behaviour that ends the partnership.

Some of these are stylistic. Most are structural. Any of them, in any unit, in any state, is grounds for take-down.

Prohibited Vocabulary
  • Never use "Casino". Not anywhere, not in any compound. (casino-style, online casino, social casino, live casino, casino app.)
  • Never use "Slot". Not anywhere, not in any compound. (slot machine, slot-style, video slots, slot game, poker slots.)
  • Never use defensive framings: "not a casino", "the legal version of slots", "better than a casino".
  • Never use RNG, random number generator, "chance-based", "luck-engine".
  • Never use "sweepstakes", "sweepstake coins", or any sweepstakes-coded mechanic language.
  • Never use "historical horse racing", "past races" or "previously run events" as product descriptors, especially in CO & KS.
Prohibited Promises
  • Never imply guaranteed winnings, risk-free wagering, "can't lose", "sure thing".
  • Never use "earn", "make money", "investment", "returns", "profit" in connection with Jokio.
  • Never claim a "house edge", "RTP" or "return to player". Those concepts don't exist in pari-mutuel.
  • Never run unapproved "free wagers", "free bets", "bonus bets" language without written sign-off.
  • Never frame the reveal as the source of winnings. The live race is. Always.
Prohibited Calls-to-Action
  • Never use "spin to win", "hit the jackpot" (without race context), "pull the lever".
  • Never frame value with "best odds", "great odds", "improve your odds". Implies a casino-style edge play.
  • Never use "play casino games" or any variant.
  • Never use urgency tactics that imply scarcity of a guaranteed outcome.
Imagery & Symbology
  • No spinning reel mechanics, even abstracted, even decorative.
  • No children's cartoon characters, toys, school motifs, playground or classroom themes.
  • No licensed children's IP. No mascots whose primary appeal is to minors.
  • No imagery that implies the platform decides outcomes (dice, RNG visuals, "lucky machine" tropes).
Licensing & Legal Misstatements
  • Never imply Jokio independently holds a state-specific gaming licence. Refer to the ORC Hub framework.
  • Never reference any non-pari-mutuel licence (sports betting, casino, lottery, sweepstakes).
  • Never modify, reformat or compress the Provider Statement or the RG Statement.
  • Never omit the New York self-exclusion line in NY-targeted creative.
Process Violations
  • Never publish before written compliance approval, even for a "small tweak" of an approved variant.
  • Never alter the approved logo, recolour the wordmark, recreate it in another typeface, or distort proportions.
  • Never push live work to creator/influencer partners without sharing this playbook and securing their sign-off.
  • Never let metadata, alt text, hashtags or video script accompaniment carry vocabulary that the visible copy avoids.

13 / Approval Process

From draft to live.

Six steps. Built to protect the brand, the agency, the partner network, and the ORC affiliate framework. Skipping any of them is a contractual issue, not an editorial one.

Step 01
Draft Submission
Submit draft creative to Vision Derby's compliance team with all source files and channel specs.
Step 02
Compliance Review
Vision Derby reviews against this playbook, the Licence Holder Guidelines, and applicable state requirements.
Step 03
ORC Review
ORC completes a full review and returns Approval, Changes Needed, or Rejection.
Step 04
Revision
Required amendments returned with specific notes. Agency revises, then resubmits cleanly for secondary review.
Step 05
Mandatory Footer T&Cs
Apply the standard footer T&C line in every ad: "Offered by AmWest through their Oregon Multi-Jurisdictional Hub License. 21+. T&Cs apply. For help with a gambling problem, please call 1-800-GAMBLER." If the $100 offer is in the ad, prepend: "Min $5, Max $100, 1x wagering, eligibility applies. Bonus non withdrawable."
Step 06
Live Monitoring
Live creative remains under ongoing review. Vision Derby or AmWest may request removal at any time.
